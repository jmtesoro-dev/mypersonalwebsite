My Goals as a Student

Here are the aspirations I’m passionately pursuing!

Short-Term Goals

Mastering programming while learning more about myself.

As a student and future front-end developer, my short-term goal is to improve my skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I want to explore my interests, turn my passion for cute and aesthetic designs into real projects, and build confidence in my profession one website at a time.

Long-Term Goals

Becoming a proficient front-end developer.

My long-term goal is to become a skilled front-end developer — my dream profession. I love programming because it lets me be both creative and logical. I want to grow from a curious student into a professional who builds beautiful, useful websites that reflect who I am.

Personal Development

Growing as a person through programming.

Programming is not just about my profession, it's about myself. It taught me patience when debugging, confidence when a project works, and enthusiasm to keep learning. My personal goal is to invest in myself — to become more disciplined, creative, and passionate, both in life and in code.

Career Goals

Turning my love for programming into my career.

My career goal is to turn my passion for programming into my profession. I want to freelance, build a strong portfolio, and someday have my own creative studio. A career where my self, my interests, and my love for coding all come together — doing what I love every day.
