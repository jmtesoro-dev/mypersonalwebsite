Skills I'm Developing

As a student, I’m actively developing a diverse set of skills that reflect my passions in technology and beyond.

Information Technology

  • I am learning front end web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I enjoy turning ideas into real websites.
  • I am practicing responsive design so my websites look good on any device.
  • My goal is to build modern and clean websites that are easy to use.

Graphic Design

  • I use tools like Canva and Figma to explore visual design. I really like cute and aesthetic layouts.
  • I am learning about colors, branding, and how to make designs look balanced.
  • I want to combine design and coding so my websites look good and work well.

Public Speaking

  • I am working on my communication skills. I used to be shy but I am trying to be more confident.
  • I practice explaining my ideas clearly, especially when presenting my projects.
  • I want to be able to share my work and my thoughts without being nervous.

Problem Solving and Critical Thinking

  • Programming taught me how to solve problems. I enjoy fixing bugs and finding solutions.
  • I try to find simple and efficient ways to make my code work better.
  • I am learning to think logically and not give up when things get complicated.
